The Expo

Where Esports Meets Kenya's Future.

The Nairobi Esports Expo is a national convening bringing the wider esports ecosystem into one space. From the player holding a controller to the infrastructure powering the game, esports connects an extraordinary range of people, industries and opportunities. The Expo creates a space to play, discover, connect and imagine what comes next.

Five Experience Pillars

🔍

Think about Kenya beyond 2030.

Who Is It For?

Gamers

Compete, connect with the community and discover opportunities beyond playing.

Families

Understand the world young people are already participating in and discover the skills, careers and communities growing around gaming.

Young People

Explore technology, creativity, skills, careers and entrepreneurship.

Creators

Connect gaming with content, media, design, entertainment and new audiences.

Educators & Institutions

Explore new ways of engaging young people through technology, skills and esports.

Businesses

Meet the community and discover where your organisation fits into the esports economy.
