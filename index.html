About the Expo

A stage for Kenyan esports — and the businesses that power it

The Nairobi Esports Expo brings together players, gaming teams, publishers, hardware and peripheral brands, telcos, and fans under one roof. It's a showcase of talent, technology, and opportunity across Kenya's fast-growing esports and gaming industry — from grassroots competitive play to professional tournaments.

Whether you're a brand looking to reach engaged gaming audiences, a team or player seeking exposure, or a fan who wants to get closer to the action, the expo is built for you.
